Here is the complete muscle-by-muscle exercise breakdown:

Chest (Pectoralis Major and Minor)
Barbell Bench Press: Lie on a flat bench, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, lower it to mid-chest, and press up. Targets overall mass and strength.

Incline Dumbbell Press: Set bench to a 30 to 45-degree angle, press dumbbells up and slightly inward. Targets the clavicular head (upper chest).

Chest Dips: Support yourself on parallel bars, lean forward slightly, lower your body until elbows reach a 90-degree angle, and push up. Targets lower chest and triceps.

Cable Crossovers: Stand between dual pulleys, bring handles together in a hugging motion across the front of your body. Targets inner chest definition and continuous tension.

Back (Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Trapezius, Erector Spinae)
Pull-Ups / Chin-Ups: Grab an overhead bar with an overhand (pull-up) or underhand (chin-up) grip, pull your chest to the bar. Targets overall upper back width and thickness.

Barbell Bent-Over Row: Hinge at the hips with a flat back, pull the barbell to your lower ribcage. Targets mid-back thickness and lats.

Seated Cable Row: Sit at a low pulley station, pull the handle to your abdomen while keeping your torso upright. Targets mid-back and rhomboids.

Lat Pulldown: Sit at a lat pulldown machine, pull the wide bar down to your upper chest. Targets lat width.

Shoulders (Anterior, Lateral, and Posterior Deltoids)
Overhead Barbell Press (Military Press): Stand or sit, press a barbell from collarbone height straight overhead. Targets overall shoulder mass and core stability.

Dumbbell Lateral Raises: Stand holding light dumbbells at your sides, raise arms out to the sides until parallel to the floor. Targets lateral deltoids for shoulder width.

Face Pulls: Using a rope attachment on a cable machine at face height, pull toward your face while separating your hands. Targets rear deltoids and upper posture.

Dumbbell Front Raises: Raise a dumbbell forward until it reaches eye level. Targets anterior (front) deltoids.

Legs (Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves)
Barbell Back Squat: Rest a barbell across your upper traps, squat down by breaking at the hips and knees until thighs are parallel to the floor, then drive up. Targets quads, glutes, and core.

Romanian Deadlift (RDL): Hold a barbell or dumbbells, hinge at the hips with a slight bend in the knees, lower the weight until a stretch is felt in the hamstrings, and return. Targets hamstrings and glutes.

Leg Press: Sit in a leg press machine, place feet shoulder-width apart on the sled, lower the weight, and press back up. Targets quadriceps.

Standing Calf Raises: Place the balls of your feet on a raised block, lower your heels for a deep stretch, and rise onto your toes. Targets the gastrocnemius.

Arms (Biceps, Triceps, Forearms)
Barbell Bicep Curl: Hold a barbell with an underhand grip, curl the weight toward your shoulders without moving your elbows. Targets bicep mass.

Hammer Curls: Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other (neutral grip), curl up. Targets brachialis and brachioradialis for arm thickness.

Tricep Skull Crushers: Lie on a flat bench holding an EZ-bar, lower the bar toward your forehead by bending only at the elbows, then extend back up. Targets overall tricep mass.

Tricep Cable Pushdowns: Use a rope or straight bar on a high pulley, push down until arms are fully locked out. Targets lateral and medial tricep heads.

Core (Rectus Abdominis, Obliques, Transverse Abdominis)
Hanging Leg Raises: Hang from an overhead bar, lift your legs straight out or bend knees toward your chest. Targets lower abs and hip flexors.

Cable Woodchoppers: Stand sideways to a cable pulley, pull the handle diagonally across your body with a rotating torso motion. Targets obliques and rotational core strength.

Plank: Hold a forearm plank position while keeping your body in a straight line and bracing your core. Targets deep core stabilizers (transverse abdominis).
